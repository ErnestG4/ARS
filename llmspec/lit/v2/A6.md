## B. Muon spectral dynamics / implicit bias → Q4, P3, rows 10 and 15

**6. Beneventano, Abdelmoneum, Poggio, "The Spectral Dynamics and Noise Geometry of Muon" (arXiv 2606.08388, Jun 2026).** Exact singular-value dynamics for continuous-time Muon (underdetermined regression): the normalised spectrum flows to **equal nonzero singular values**, distinct from nuclear-norm minimisation; NanoGPT: Muon **preserves stable rank** over a broad lr plateau; a small-ViT control reverses the ranking (regime-dependent). Theory + small empirical. Bears on row 16, P3. ALREADY PUBLISHED (endpoint); our dense-trajectory **timing** of the collapse is not in it. Changes plan? n — cite beside Moonlight §3.4.

**7. Kang, Yun, Shin, Cho, Yun, "Uniform Spectral Growth … of Muon in LoRA-Style Matrix Factorization" (arXiv 2602.06385, Feb 2026).** Under Muon all singular values of the product grow at one rate and **small ones reach target first** — the reverse of gradient flow's largest-first stepwise learning (the Boix-Adserà seed). Theory + toy. Bears on rows 10, 15, lead 8. SUGGESTS NEW TEST: stable rank of W−W₀ over steps, A0 vs M0s1 (‖W−W₀‖_F is banked; its rank is not). Prediction: incremental rank growth under AdamW, none under Muon. Changes plan? **y** (one column in the Q4EXT descriptive script).

**8. Fan, Schmidt, Thrampoulidis (NeurIPS 2025 spotlight, arXiv 2502.04664):** Muon → max-margin in **spectral norm**, Adam → in **max-norm** (linear multiclass). **Tsilivis, Gronich, Kempe, Vardi, "Flavors of Margin" (arXiv 2410.22069):** steepest descent under any norm in homogeneous nets → KKT point of that norm's margin problem; Adam/Shampoo experiments. Theory. Mechanism behind "flatter Muon spectra" (P3). CONFIRMS. Changes plan? n.

**9. Wu, Yu, Zhang, Woodland, "Spectral Allocation" (arXiv 2608.25990, Aug 2026).** Decomposing the momentum buffer into singular directions and fitting the optimal step per direction on held-out data gives a profile **stable across batches and training stages**: an edge-of-stability "head" needs tiny steps, the "bulk" tolerates large ones; Muon wins by using the bulk (124M–1B). Empirical/scale. Bears on PDYN P4 and the M4 Gram hook. SUGGESTS: the head/bulk split is a published update statistic the hook could estimate. Changes plan? n (M4 off pending its A/B).

**10. ## D. Dyson Brownian motion for weights → PDYN reference family

**14. Aarts et al. DBM (arXiv 2407.16427, 2411.13512) — v1-listed; plus "From SGD to Spectra" (arXiv 2507.12709, Jul 2025; authors not captured by the fetch — UNVERIFIED) and Park, Lucini, Aarts (arXiv 2509.01349, Sep 2025).** The two newer papers: squared singular values follow DBM with gamma-type "bulk + tail" stationary densities, validated on transformer/MLP; a phase diagram in (init variance, lr/batch) with three dynamical regimes. All are **SGD without momentum** per the abstracts (momentum treatment UNVERIFIED). So PDYN_PREREG_A1's driver-matched family is a genuine extension, and the literature's lr/batch "temperature" gives a sealed-able P4 prediction (velocity rms ∝ √(lr/B)) for the per-step pilot. Changes plan? **y** — cite this family as the reference in PDYN_PREREG_A1 and state what A1 adds; no verdict change.
