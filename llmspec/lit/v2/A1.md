## 0. Direct answer: update-rank trajectories on Pythia/PolyPythias or dense 70M–1B checkpoints?

**Not as such.** Nearest items:

- **Diehl Martinez, Lesci, Buttery, EMNLP-Findings 2024 (arXiv 2410.11451)** — Pythia 70M/160M/410M/1.4B/2.8B at steps 0, {1,2,…,512}, 1k, 3k, then every 10k. Entropy effective rank (PER) of the WRITE matrices only (attn O, MLP_OUT) and of their **one-batch gradients** at each checkpoint; not ΔW. Gradient PER "slightly decreases throughout training" at every size; small models' late-layer weight PER falls over training, large models' stabilises early. The only Pythia-checkpoint gradient-rank trajectory found.
- **Thomas 2026 (arXiv 2607.05872)** — gradient top-r subspace on a pretrained Pythia-160M checkpoint and own Pythia-1B-architecture runs (70M–6.9B, four families): the top-128 gradient subspace is **non-identifiable beyond k* ≈ 39 reproducible directions**; two same-step estimates from disjoint minibatches disagree as much as estimates T steps apart; spectrum = small spike (few–~20 dirs) + gapless power-law tail shrinking only as N^(-1/4) under averaging. Not a ΔW trajectory but the key instrument caveat (§3).
- **Shivagunde et al. 2026 (arXiv 2605.13652)** — own LLaMA-style 60M/130M/350M on C4, five checkpoints per scale (~12k–60k at 350M); effective rank, stable rank, spectral gap, count(σ>0.1) of **consecutive-checkpoint ΔW**, full-rank vs five low-rank methods. Full-rank update rank "relatively stable, high"; no early regime.
- **Weiss, Africa, Buttery, Diehl Martinez 2025 (arXiv 2509.12960)** — own Llama-style 11M/66M on Dolma, 20k steps; PER and condition number of gradient updates (O, V, SwiGLU W₂); full-rank update PER "relatively flat".
- **Nobody measures ΔW stable rank on PolyPythias or an early (<1k-step) update-rank trajectory at 70M–1B.** Arm B Q3 and memo row 10 are unoccupied — but see §3.

## 2. Reading against our rows

**Row 15 / Q3.** WeLore (Q/K/O/gate low-rank fast; V and MLP up/down not) and Biderman (MLP ΔW higher-rank than attention) independently predict the Arm B type split. "Q/K early updates 5–7× lower-rank, V/O/MLP not" is the expected pattern; the sealed all-type bar asked a question the field answers "type-dependent".

**Row 10.** ΔW rank rising with interval/training is consistent with Biderman (7B, coarse) and ReLoRA (350M, 15k steps). Diehl Martinez's falling **gradient** PER on Pythia is a different object (one-batch gradient vs accumulated ΔW); the memo should say so.

**Q/K stable-rank collapse (memo §2, Q4).** Kobayashi et al. supply the missing mechanism candidate: AdamW decay on W_KᵀW_Q is an early-acting nuclear-norm penalty. Chen & Luo put key-query collapse in a second stage after other matrices condense (matches OV-before-QK). Muon's different interaction with decay is a candidate for row 16's delayed, shallower collapse.

**Boix-Adserà seed.** Empirical support is own-run ViT and GPT-2/Wikitext-103 (InRank adds GPT-2). Nobody has checked incremental rank on a public dense-checkpoint suite.

## 3. Instrument warning (new)

Thomas 2026: the minibatch gradient of Pythia-class models has a gapless power-law bulk with ~39/128 reproducible top directions. A short-interval ΔW under Adam is a whitened sum of such gradients, so **ΔW stable rank at [100,130] may be bulk-dominated**; the early/late ratio then partly measures how much Adam noise has averaged out — interval-length and LR dependent, which is the commensurability caveat of ARMB_FINDINGS §4 restated as a mechanism. Before any Q3 re-read: split-half ΔW (disjoint data halves or interleaved steps), report reproducible rank k* beside stable rank, and plant a rank-k update to show k is recovered.
