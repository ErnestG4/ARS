# LIT REVIEW v2 — A2: how much of the trained weights is still the initialisation (2026-10-02)

Scope: distance from init, lazy vs rich, weight-decay shrinkage of the init, random-feature readings of trained bulks. Bears on ARMA_PREREG_SKELETON §0 H_RES, BULK_INIT_OVERLAP_PREREG.md, memo row 11. Every item was fetched (arXiv abs/HTML, GitHub raw, or PDF text); UNVERIFIED marks what could not be read from the source. De-duplicated against FINDINGS_MEMO.md and the coordinator's v1 paper list (the v1 file was lost); v1 papers appear only where this review adds a NEW use, marked [v1].

## 0. KEY QUESTION
**No published measurement of the trained BULK's overlap with the step-0 instance was found**, in transformers or elsewhere. The literature is (a) distributional — does the bulk follow MP / Wigner / Porter–Thomas (Martin–Mahoney; Thamm–Staats–Rosenow 2022; Staats–Thamm–Rosenow 2024 on Pythia-410M, Llama-3.1-8B, BERT) — which tests the LAW, not the instance; (b) whole-matrix or per-element (embedding cosines to step 0; sign persistence); (c) theory for GD at small LR (bulk spectrum invariant; nothing on bulk eigenvectors). ρ_bulk and E_init as defined in the prereg §2 appear to be new. The nearest confusable the prereg must beat is (a): MP-agreement of W_O does not imply W_O's bulk is W₀'s bulk.

## 2. Init fraction under decoupled AdamW (prereg α_wd, E_init)
- **Mechanics (configs/source fetched):** Pythia: `weight-decay 0.1`, cosine to 0.1·lr, 1 % warmup, 143 000 steps; gpt-neox `Adam` → APEX/DeepSpeed FusedAdam, `adam_w_mode=True` by default (decoupled, lr-multiplied). The prereg's λ = 0.1 is right; α_wd(t) is exact up to gpt-neox's cosine formula (~0.01).
- **Computed α_wd(143k):** 70M 0.0004, 160M 0.009, 410M/1B 0.095, 1.4B 0.21, 2.8B 0.28, 6.9B/12B 0.39. Trajectory 1.4B: 0.94 (4k), 0.87 (8k), 0.74 (16k), 0.55 (32k), 0.33 (64k), 0.21; 410M: 0.91, 0.80, 0.63, 0.40, 0.19, 0.095. Arm-B 70M runs have no init amplitude left; 1.4B and the 410M seeds differ 2× — report separately.
- **E_init is energy, not amplitude.** Kosson's equilibrium puts the UPDATE part at rms √(η/2λ) ≈ 0.032 per element (1.4B) vs small_init σ = √(2/5d) ≈ 0.014; surviving init ≈ 0.21·0.014 ≈ 0.003. Near equilibrium, E_init ≈ 1 % and ρ ≈ 0.1 despite 21 % surviving amplitude. ∫ηλ ≈ 1.57 timescales (410M: 2.35), so equilibrium is only partly reached, and the bulk's share of update energy is unknown — a back-of-envelope, not a result. It does place INIT-DOMINATED (ρ ≥ 0.80) far from the mechanistic expectation: that word would be a genuine surprise.
