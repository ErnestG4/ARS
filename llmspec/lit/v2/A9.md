## A. Many correlated cells (ARMB_FINDINGS §6, §8 lead 8; memo row 1)

1. **Meinshausen, Maathuis, Bühlmann 2011, Ann. Stat. (arXiv 1106.2068).** Westfall–Young min-p/max-T permutation is asymptotically optimal for FWER under block dependence + sparsity; strong control under subset pivotality for any dependence; 20–250% more power than Bonferroni–Holm. | theory + sim | lead 8 | SUGGESTS NEW TEST | **y**: the statistic should be max over cells of |d|/SD with a resampled null, not a per-cell MDD.
2. **Romano & Wolf 2005, JASA 100:94–108.** Stepdown FWER without subset pivotality; "for time series or dependent-data situations, block bootstrap methods should be used (Lahiri 2003)". | theory | lead 8 (checkpoint correlation) | SUGGESTS NEW TEST | **y**: resample checkpoint *blocks* within the reference runs, never single checkpoints.
3. **Blanchard, Neuvial, Roquain 2020, Ann. Stat. 48(3):1281–1303; R `sanssouci`.** Post hoc confidence bound on the number of false positives in *any* user-selected set (joint FWER over a reference family), λ-calibrated by permutation under unknown dependence. | theory + software | lead 8 ("count statistic … family-wise MDD") | SUGGESTS NEW TEST | **y**: this is the count statistic with a measured null the lead asks for, and it stays valid when a reader picks cells after the fact (sign runs, V at 550–600).
4. **Maris & Oostenveld 2007, J. Neurosci. Methods 164:177–190.** Cluster-based permutation: adjacent exceedances summed into a cluster mass, null = permutation distribution of the max cluster mass. | method | lead 8 scatter ("one excursion, not three") | SUGGESTS NEW TEST | **y**: makes the adjacency argument a statistic. **Caveat — Sassenhagen & Draschkow 2019, Psychophysiology (PMID 30657176):** a cluster p licenses existence only, never onset or extent. **Rousselet 2025, Eur. J. Neurosci. 61(1):e16618:** FDR, max-statistic and cluster-depth do no better: an onset is a second-order (interaction) property no cell-wise method tests. | sim | rows 13–14 | CONFIRMS that timing cannot be read off cell-wise tests.

*Synthesis for lead 8 (mine):* bank d for every cell; statistics (a) max |d|/SD, (b) exceedance count, (c) max adjacent-checkpoint cluster mass; null = the same statistics on the 10 AdamW reference runs under circular block bootstrap over checkpoints; red-path a planted departure of declared size, cell count and run length.

## B. Multimodality tests (memo row 3; OLMO_PREMISE_PREREG T1–T4)

5. **Cheng & Hall 1998, JRSS-B 60:579–589.** Uniform-calibrated dip/excess-mass tests (Hartigan & Hartigan 1985, Ann. Stat. 13:70–84) have asymptotic level **zero** at every nominal level; Silverman's bandwidth test is also conservative (York: asymptotic levels 0.000/0.010/0.032/0.102 at nominal 0.01/0.05/0.1/0.2); bootstrap calibration from a fitted unimodal density restores level and wins power where modes are close. | theory + sim | row 3 ("misses minority or broad peaks"); T1/T4 | **CONTRADICTS** the implicit nominal size of our `dip p < 0.01` rule: a 1% per-head null rate and ~0% power at a 20%/sd-0.1 peak are what an undersized test gives | **y**: run a calibrated excess-mass test beside the dip on every T1/T4 head.
6. **Ameijeiras-Alonso, Crujeiras, Rodríguez-Casal 2019, TEST 28:900–919 (arXiv 1609.05188); `multimode`, JSS 2021 (arXiv 1803.00472).** ACR = excess mass calibrated by bootstrap from the critical-bandwidth KDE; "the dip test presents an extremely conservative behaviour"; only HY (known support), CH and ACR have asymptotically correct level; `modetest(method="ACR", lowsup=…)` and `sizer` ship in the package. | theory + sim + software | row 3; T2 licence class | SUGGESTS NEW TEST | **y**: singular values have a known lower bound 0, so the known-support ACR/HY variants apply, and the dead-row trim becomes a declared support choice.
7. **Freeman & Dale 2013 (in v1 for its FPR numbers).** New here: their grid is separation 1–8 SD × minority weight 0–50% × skew × n, and for the dip minority weight has a strong effect (β −.44, vs −.59 for separation) while positive skew *inflates* detections. | sim | OLMO prereg §4 single power cell (d 0.3, sd 0.03, w 0.5: the easiest corner) | CONFIRMS our ~0% power at 20% weight | **y**: seal a (weight, separation, skew) power grid plus a right-skewed-unimodal FPR cell (our nearest confusable).

## C. Change points on smooth curves (memo rows 13, 14)

8. **Fryzlewicz 2023, JASA (DOI 10.1080/01621459.2023.2211733), NSP; R `nsp`.** Finite-sample, unconditional guarantee: with prob ≥ 1−α every returned interval contains a change in local linear-model parameters, and under no change the probability of returning *any* interval is ≤ α; covers kinks and autoregression; simulation-based thresholds allowed. | theory | row 13 NOT LICENSED detector | SUGGESTS NEW TEST | **y**, conditionally: first candidate with a built-in false-CP bound, but the bound is against a *locally linear* null; a curved smooth curve is still flagged at fine scales, so its false-CP rate on our curves must be measured first, as row 13 did.
9. **Baranowski, Chen, Fryzlewicz 2019, JRSS-B 81:649–672 (NOT); Fearnhead & Grose, JSS 109(7) (CPOP).** On non-piecewise-constant signals a single-change least-squares fit lands *between* true kinks (BS/WBS fail); heterogeneous noise manufactures false change points. | theory + sim | row 13 | CONFIRMS: a mean-shift detector on smooth curves is the wrong model class | n.
10. **Chaudhuri & Marron 1999, JASA 94:807–823, SiZer.** Simultaneous CIs for the derivative of the *smoothed* curve across all bandwidths; a turning point is significant at scale h when the derivative is significantly positive on one side and negative on the other; ESS-based multiplicity adjustment; dependent-SiZer (Park, Marron, Rondonotti 2004) for autocorrelated errors. | theory + method | rows 13–14 ("~2k turning points") | SUGGESTS NEW TEST | **y**: replaces "change point" with "significant zero-crossing at scale h", giving the turning point a scale-indexed location error bar and answering whether the 2k feature exists at coarse resolution or only at fine.

## D. Pre-registration practice in ML (all PREREG files)

11. **Hofman, Chatzimparmpas, Sharma, Watts, Hullman 2023 (arXiv 2311.18807).** Two-phase prereg (A before fitting, B before the test set), time-stamped via OSF/AsPredicted; Phase B must list changes to Phase A. The NeurIPS 2020/21 pre-registration workshops (PMLR v148; preregister.science) allow changes only if it is "made clear which protocols have been modified after observing the evidence". | position + practice | PDYN_PREREG → A1; "what counts as a seal" | CONFIRMS commit = seal (a pushed hash is a third-party timestamp) | n; add one amendment field: "what had been seen when this was written".
12. **Jankowsky, Speck, Cummins, Schroeders, Nature Human Behaviour correspondence, 10 Sept 2026 (s41562-026-02586-2).** Registered Reports for ML prediction incl. LLMs; when the setting space is too large to fix upfront, prespecify a *calibration–confirmation split* (sandbox partition for choosing settings, one-shot evaluation on the rest); Stage-1 review before test performance informs decisions. | practice | PDYN phases 2–3, Q4EXT, pilots vs banked arms | SUGGESTS | **y**: name our pilot/banked-arm structure as calibration–confirmation in each prereg and forbid banked-arm reads during calibration explicitly.

## E. Statistics for n = 1 or 2 paired runs (row 14 A0/A0r floor; row 16 Muon pair)

13. **Fehlauer, Mahowald, Pimentel, EMNLP 2025 (DOI 10.18653/v1/2025.emnlp-main.1675).** Across-seed per-token KL on PolyPythias (in v1) runs through uniform, sharp-convergence, sharp-*divergence* and slow-reconvergence phases; small models never reconverge. | empirical | row 14 floor; PDYN "n = 1 at the same step" | SUGGESTS | **y**: the between-run floor is time-varying, so a divergence floor is stated per step (as PDYN_PREREG does), never as one scalar across a warp. Side note: the ten PolyPythia-70M runs are a free seed-reference distribution for 70M bulk statistics (lead 8 null), not an identical-config floor for A0.
14. **Madaan et al. 2024 (arXiv 2406.10229).** Ten identical Llama-2-7B seeds × 21 checkpoints; a training-choice difference counts only if it exceeds seed variance. | empirical/scale | row 16 | CONFIRMS the design | n.
15. **Jordan, ICLR 2024 (arXiv 2304.01910).** Runs make ≈independent test errors; changing a *single weight* at init gives 7–7.5% prediction disagreement vs 8.5% for fully different seeds. | empirical | row 14 NOT RESOLVABLE | CONFIRMS | n: an identical-config rerun behaves like a new seed for trajectory statistics, so the A0/A0r floor is not small and one pair cannot estimate its own spread.
16. **Bouthillier et al. 2021, MLSys; Agarwal et al. 2021, NeurIPS (arXiv 2108.13264).** Data order etc. exceed init variance, so randomise all sources and compare paired; in the few-run regime report interval estimates (stratified bootstrap, IQM), since 3–5-run point estimates already mislead. | empirical/method | row 16 (two Muon runs) | CONFIRMS | **y**: with n = 2 no distributional claim is licensed; phrase Q4 as a reference-band test (both Muon runs outside the band of the 10 AdamW seed runs at the same step), with the band's n = 10 stated.
