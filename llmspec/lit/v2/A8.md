## Headline answer to the arm-A question
Yes: cost-vs-spectral-position curves exist, and the closest is on **Pythia-410m-deduped** (Staats, Thamm & Rosenow, arXiv 2410.17770, NeurIPS 2025). Zeroing one rank-decile in every layer of a type gives: **Q/K/V monotone decreasing** down the spectrum (top decile dominant, impact "lessens as we move down"); **Attention-Output ≈ flat/insensitive** beyond the top values ("closer to random initialization, mainly affected by regularization"); **MLP Up/Down U-shaped** — the bottom decile costs more than intermediate deciles. Nobody has: octave bands, rotation-vs-value families, dose-linearity checks, antithetic pairs, or a co-adapted calibrator. Arm A is novel on all of those; the published shapes are its prior.

## Papers

**1. Staats, Thamm, Rosenow — Small Singular Values Matter (arXiv 2410.17770, v1 2024-10, v3 2025-11; NeurIPS 2025).**
Claim: in BERT, Pythia-410m, Llama-3.1-8B, spectral regions outside MP (both ends) have singular vectors with high overlap (O_k>0.5) with activation-covariance eigenvectors; zeroing those deciles raises perplexity most; W_O stays MP-like. Trajectory (Pythia, block 10, Fig. 5): large-σ overlap present by step 1000 for every type; W_O **loses** it later; **small-σ overlap appears only late in pretraining** (Down-proj) and "increases drastically" (Up-proj). Mechanism offered: in non-square matrices small σ re-weight directions, so small-σ function is architectural. BoolQ: removing the bottom decile before fine-tuning is harmless, after it is severe. | empirical, 410M–8B | row 11, lead 7, MF_EXPLORE §3, ARMA §2/§6 | **CONFIRMS** (bulk not inert; tail functional) and **ALREADY PUBLISHED** (a decile cost curve on our substrate) | **Plan change: y.** (a) Their Down-proj small-σ overlap is on the right singular vectors = neuron side, the same side/band where MF_EXPLORE §3 sees late localisation. (b) The U-shape is not in ARMA §6's fit family (constant / power / broken power / cutoff): add a rising-tail model or seal MF_EXPLORE §6's "deepest band reported separately" rule. (c) Their cost is per zeroed decile, neither size- nor output-matched, so "bottom decile matters" is a per-unit-‖δW‖ statement — exactly the C^raw vs C^out split. (d) Type priors: O → RESERVOIR-like, Q/K/V → decreasing, MLP → knee at the bottom. Record as prior art, not predictions.

**2. Sharma, Ash, Misra — LASER (arXiv 2312.13558, 2023-12; GPT-J, Llama-2-7B, RoBERTa).**
Removing up to ~99% of the rank of late-layer **MLP input** matrices (4096 → 4) improves CounterFact accuracy; attention gives minimal gain; the curve is non-monotone (improves, then collapses); removed components "encode the correct semantic type but the incorrect response" or high-frequency tokens; **PILE perplexity rises 4.8 → 5.0** as accuracy rises. | empirical, 6–7B | row 11, lead 7 | **CONFIRMS** bulk carries function (loss cost positive; the gain is a task effect) | n — arm A's probe-loss readout is right; a task metric can invert the sign.

**3. Staats, Thamm, Rosenow — Boundary between noise and information (arXiv 2206.03927; PRE 108, L022302, 2023).**
Porter–Thomas comparison of singular vectors locates a noise/information boundary; filtering (remove small σ, shrink large σ to undo level repulsion) **improves** generalisation for CNN/MLP trained with label noise. | empirical, small vision nets | MF_EXPLORE §2, §5 | **ALREADY PUBLISHED** (PT as the boundary) and **CONTRADICTS** "tail matters" under label noise: the sign of tail cost is regime-dependent | n — §7 line: the calibrator's own tail-cost sign can be negative; report it.

**6. Guth, Ménard, Rochette, Mallat — A Rainbow in Deep Network Black Boxes (arXiv 2305.18512 v3 2024-10).**
Trained deep CNNs approximately satisfy the rainbow hypothesis: each layer's weights ≈ random features drawn with the trained per-layer weight covariance (low-rank from feature learning); resampled networks match trained performance. | empirical + theory, CNNs | ARMA §0 H_RES, §4 | **CONFIRMS** H_RES is a live published model, not a straw man | **Plan change: y (minor).** A second calibrator: resample the bulk from the trained covariance (rainbow) rather than Haar-rotate within the bulk subspace (which holds σ exactly). ## What is NOT in the literature (as fetched)
No study perturbs trained weights by spectral **band** against a co-adapted calibrator; none separates value- from direction-perturbation; none checks dose² linearity; none runs along a pretraining trajectory except #1's overlap curve (one block). #1's decile curves are the nearest confusable to a positive arm-A result: a decreasing or U-shaped cost by rank index is **already expected** without self-similarity, so SELF-SIMILAR must mean "exponent ≠ calibrator's" (ARMA §6 says so; #1 makes the rule load-bearing).
