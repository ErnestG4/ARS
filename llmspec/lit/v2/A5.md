## 3. RMT theory for the null and for τ(q)

**Alt, Erdős, Krüger, EJP 22 (2017), arXiv 1606.07353 (PDF text) [v1; new here: the licence conditions].** Gram matrices with arbitrary variance profile S: under flatness (A) s_ik ≤ s*/(p+n) AND lower bound (F2) s_ik ≥ φ/(n+p), bulk local law holds and "the standard methods … can be used to prove an anisotropic law and delocalization of eigenvectors in the bulk". Theorem. Our null G = c·D_r Z D_c IS such a matrix: **its bulk vectors are delocalised by theorem only while BOTH bounds hold**; dead rows / strongly heterogeneous norms violate (F2) — exactly the disclosed "null's top vectors localise by construction". **CONFIRMS + names the licence.** Changes plan: **y** — record max/min row- and column-norm² per matrix; bands where the null itself leaves Haar are null-INAPPLICABLE, not trained-matrix evidence (the spike −5…−13 and the negative Q/K [32,64) cells are candidates).

**Benigni, AIHP 56 (2020), arXiv 1711.07103; Marcinek & Yau, CMP (2022), arXiv 2005.08425.** Deformed Wigner / generalised Rosenzweig–Porter: eigenvector entries asymptotically Gaussian with an explicit position-dependent variance, Cauchy (Lorentzian) for a well-spread initial spectrum; joint Gaussianity for general Wigner-type (variance-profile) matrices. Theorems. Bears on §2/§5: head-nested tails 3–6× above PT that the null TRACKS are Gaussian-with-profile, not localisation; RMT counterpart of Karkada et al.'s Lorentzian amplitudes (seed). **CONFIRMS.** n — but state the comparison as "Gaussian entries with the null's profile", not PT.

**Kravtsov, Khaymovich, Cuevas, Amini, NJP 17, 122002 (2015), arXiv 1508.01714; Kutlin & Khaymovich, SciPost Phys. 16, 008 (2024), arXiv 2309.06468; Biroli & Tarzia, PRB 103, 104205 (2021), arXiv 2012.12841; Das, Ghosh, Khaymovich, PRB 112, 024201 (2025), arXiv 2501.17242.** RP: fractal non-ergodic phase for 1 < γ < 2 (D_q flat in q). Kutlin–Khaymovich: "no random matrix model with a statistically homogeneous distribution of diagonal disorder and uncorrelated off-diagonal terms can host a multifractal phase" — genuine multifractality needs structure in the on-site energies; Lévy-RP (fat-tailed hopping) gives a weakly multifractal NEE phase (μ-range UNVERIFIED, abstract only); power-law-decaying eigenstates give multifractality without fine tuning. Theory + numerics. Bears on arm B's τ(q): "structured part + Gaussian bulk at a smaller scale" is RP-type, so a FRACTAL (q-flat) band near the spike edge is the nearest confusable; §3's shape (q = 1.5–2 ≈ 0, q = 3–4 explode) is the opposite — a few localised vectors, not a fractal band. **SUGGESTS design constraint**: declare three τ(q) shapes — fractal (flat D_q), multifractal (curved, and only with a size sweep), few-vector (high-q only) — with the RP line as the confusable. Changes plan: **y** (exploratory design).

**Rodriguez, Vasquez, Römer, PRL 102, 106406 (2009), arXiv 0812.1654 (PDF text).** f(α) from the PDF of coarse-grained |ψ|² (boxes l = 5, effective size L/l), symmetry relation f(2d−α) = f(α)+d−α, agreement with gIPR box-size scaling; √ln L finite-size term. Numerics. For arm B's fixed-n box moments: f(α) from one size, symmetry relation as a free self-consistency check. SUGGESTS. n.

**Aggarwal, Lopatto, Marcinek, Ann. Probab. 49(4) (2021), arXiv 2002.09355.** Lévy matrices: small-energy eigenvectors delocalised yet entries NON-Gaussian (normal × eigenvalue-dependent scale; inverse one-sided stable for the median). Theorem. Bears on §2/§5: heavy-tailed weight entries (HT-SR regime, rare-token neurons) give PT departures WITHOUT localisation, which the Gaussian null cannot represent. **SUGGESTS NEW CONTROL**: one extra norm-preserving null with the trained matrix's own entry distribution (entry-shuffled Z, or sign-flip × permutation inside the profile). Changes plan: **y** (one extra draw; cheap).

**Zhu & Kunisky, arXiv 2602.15994 (2026)** (theorem): all eigenvectors of variance-profile Wigner matrices decorrelate under entry resampling, explicit thresholds — a theory null for A0↔A0r bulk-vector overlap (row 14, PDYN M3). **Osman, arXiv 2609.11630 (2026)** (theorem): max eigenvector entry Gumbel-distributed — a per-vector EXISTENCE statistic for §3's "few vectors carry it" and §5's tail fraction. Both SUGGEST; n.
