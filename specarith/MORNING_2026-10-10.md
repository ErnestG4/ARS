- **Design concern (decision for Will).**
  - **The constant-c∞ picture looks wrong.** The tapered F1 data decline roughly logarithmically in b: F1a
    v(b) = 0.066, 0.052, 0.039, 0.033, 0.026 at b = 10², 3·10², 10³, 3·10³, 10⁴, about −0.02 per decade. Declared
    v ≈ K + c∞·b (G_S_DESIGN §3) is not that shape.
  - **What a log-like decline needs:** low-frequency spectral weight in C_non from near-diagonal pairs of large primes
    (p ≈ q). That weight can grow logarithmically with the prime cutoff, so the long-range integral may not converge in
    pmax the way pointwise values do.

    the direct-quadrature route needs a principled treatment of the large-prime near-diagonal sum (a Heisenberg-type
    cutoff), and Stage 2 may be better served by the few-prime/form-factor model (the declared fallback) or a
    frequency-domain computation.
